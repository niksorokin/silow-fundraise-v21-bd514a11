Why not just Claude or GPT?

Use the best model. Keep the asset. Silow runs on top of the models, and lets you change which one.

01Round

Your ownership

The asset stays with the company, not with the model vendor: the workflows, the approval history, the test sets.

The round defines where it lives and how you take it with you.

02Round

Your model independence

Silow is neutral. Plug in any model. Your test set shows whether results got worse.

Not open-source for its own sake. Rerunning the test set on a new model is roadmap.

03Round

Runs in your environment

16% of the round goes to running Silow in the client’s environment.

For legal and finance teams, that is a question of being allowed to use it at all.

In our view

A model vendor’s commercial interest is its own model. Silow’s interest is your work, on whichever model does it best.
